## What the geometry is and is not

This model is a reference reconstruction assembled from published references and
explicitly labeled engineering estimates. See [ASSUMPTIONS.md](ASSUMPTIONS.md)
and [GAPS.md](GAPS.md) for what is measured, what is inferred, and what is a
placeholder. It is **not** a manufacturing drawing package, it is not
dimensionally authoritative, and parts named `*_envelope` are provisional
volumes rather than real geometry. Do not use it to make replacement parts.
